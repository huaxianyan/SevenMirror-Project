## 中文简短说明

在电脑查看和处理选定的 Android 手机通知，支持原通知提供的操作与回复。

## 中文详细说明

SevenMirror 将你选择的 Android 手机通知同步到浏览器，让你在电脑继续查看和处理消息。

查看手机通知

- 在电脑收到同步通知，查看标题、正文和通知来源
- 在扩展列表中区分新通知与已查看通知，并按来源手机筛选
- 打开通知详情，查看原通知提供的可用操作

在电脑继续处理

- 使用原通知提供的操作，例如标记为已读
- 原通知支持回复时，直接在电脑输入并发送回复
- 点击电脑系统通知，在独立的小窗口中查看详情和回复
- 为常用处理方式设置快捷操作规则

选择同步范围

在 Android 端选择需要同步的应用和接收浏览器。
新批准的浏览器需要加入接收设备选择后，才会收到该手机后续同步的通知。
你可以按需要暂停同步，或调整应用和设备选择。

通过自己的服务连接

SevenMirror 需要配套的 Android 应用和可访问的 SevenMirror 服务。
你可以自行部署服务，也可以使用你信任的运营者提供的服务，再按配对流程连接设备。
通知与操作内容采用端到端加密传输，由获准的接收设备解密。
浏览器会在本地保存通知和必要的连接状态，部分本地数据以明文保存。

使用条件

- Android 端需要通知访问权限，并保持允许同步的运行状态
- 通知操作和回复能力取决于原应用提供的通知功能
- 手机和浏览器需要能够连接配置的服务
- 收到通知的时间受设备网络、系统后台限制和服务状态影响

项目与下载：https://github.com/huaxianyan/SevenMirror-Project

## English short description

View selected Android notifications on your computer and use supported actions and replies.

## English detailed description

SevenMirror brings notifications from your selected Android apps to your browser, so you can keep viewing and handling messages on your computer.

View phone notifications

- Receive mirrored notifications on your computer and view their titles, text, and sources
- Distinguish new notifications from those you have opened, and filter the list by source phone
- Open notification details to see the actions offered by the original notification

Continue on your computer

- Use supported notification actions, such as marking a message as read
- Type and send a reply when the original notification supports replies
- Click a desktop notification to view its details and reply in a separate small window
- Set up quick-action rules for common tasks

Choose what to sync

Select the apps and receiving browsers in the Android app.
A newly approved browser must be added to the phone's receiving-device selection before it receives subsequent mirrored notifications.
You can pause syncing or change your app and device selections as needed.

Connect through your own service

SevenMirror requires the companion Android app and an accessible SevenMirror service.
You can deploy the service yourself or use one provided by an operator you trust, then follow the pairing process to connect your devices.
Notification and action content is end-to-end encrypted in transit and decrypted by authorized receiving devices.
The browser stores notifications and necessary connection state locally. Some local data is stored in plaintext.

Requirements

- The Android app needs notification access and permission to keep running for syncing
- Available actions and replies depend on the original app's notification features
- Both the phone and browser must be able to reach the configured service
- Delivery timing depends on connectivity, operating-system background limits, and service availability
